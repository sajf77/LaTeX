\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
	In this research, we investigate coefficient bounds for various families of holomorphic multivalent functions, with a particular focus on $p$-valent, starlike, convex, close-to-convex, and bi-univalent function classes. We begin by reviewing fundamental concepts of analytic, univalent, and biunivalent functions, along with their classical subclasses. The study extends to specialized subclasses of $p$-valent functions and explores their geometric properties through differential subordination and convolution techniques. For each class, we derive sharp coefficient inequalities that provide insight into their structural behavior and geometric constraints. These results contribute to a deeper understanding of multivalent function theory and have potential applications in geometric function theory, complex analysis, and related areas in mathematical research.
